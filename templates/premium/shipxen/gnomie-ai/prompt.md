## SUMMARY

GNOMIE AI IS A BRIGHT, FRIENDLY SAAS PRODUCT LANDING PAGE FOR AN "AI-POWERED
GARDEN DESIGN AND LANDSCAPING" APP. IT IS A SINGLE LONG SCROLLING PAGE BUILT
FROM SHIPIXEN / PAGE UI LANDING-PAGE COMPONENTS: A TRANSPARENT FLOATING HEADER
(LOGO + NAV + SEARCH + DARK-MODE TOGGLE + "GET SHIPIXEN" CTA), A HERO WITH A
GRADIENT BACKDROP AND A FRAMED DASHBOARD VIDEO MOCKUP, A "MADE WITH GNOMIE"
AVATAR-MARQUEE SOCIAL-PROOF CAROUSEL, TWO ALTERNATING PRODUCT-FEATURE SECTIONS
(LIGHT "SUPERB GARDEN DESIGNS" AND DARK "SAVE $1000S") EACH WITH A FEATURE LIST
AND VIDEO, A FOUR-CARD "YOUR GARDEN, REIMAGINED" BEFORE/AFTER GRID, A SALE CTA
WITH A SECOND AVATAR MARQUEE, A "GARDENERS LOVE GNOMIE" TESTIMONIAL GRID, AN
"AFFORDABLE PLANS" PRICING SECTION WITH A MONTHLY/ANNUALLY TOGGLE AND TWO TIERS,
A FINAL "TRANSFORM YOUR GARDEN TODAY" CTA, A "FAQ" COLLAPSIBLE ACCORDION ON A
PINK PANEL, AND THE SHIPIXEN FOOTER. THE PAGE SUPPORTS LIGHT/DARK THEMES, HAS
HOVER MICRO-INTERACTIONS THROUGHOUT, AND FADE/SLIDE-UP ENTRANCE ANIMATIONS AS
SECTIONS SCROLL INTO VIEW.

THE ONLY IN-TEMPLATE PAGE IS THE LANDING PAGE ITSELF (`index.html`). EVERY NAV
AND FOOTER LINK THAT LEAVES THE TEMPLATE POINTS AT THE REAL SHIPIXEN MARKETING
SITE (FEATURES / DOCS / PRICING / BLOG / LEGAL); THOSE ARE PRESERVED AS OUTBOUND
LINKS, NOT CLONED AS ADDITIONAL PAGES.
