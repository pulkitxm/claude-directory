I'm Spencer Sharp. I live in New York City, where I design the future.

I've loved making things for as long as I can remember, and wrote my first program when I was 6 years old, just two weeks after my mom brought home the brand new Macintosh that I had asked for my birthday, despite being way too young to have one.

The only thing I loved more than computers as a kid was space. When I was 8, I climbed the backyard tree to be closer to the sky, and I've been looking up ever since. I'm passionate about the idea of humans becoming an interplanetary species, and I've spent my career working on technologies that will help make that happen.

Today, I'm the founder and CEO of Planetaria, where we're working on the first space travel company that doesn't require you to sell a kidney to afford the trip. We're focused on passenger comfort and safety, and we're making huge strides on our orbital mechanics simulator.

Outside of work, I enjoy hiking in the Hudson Valley, photography with my Leica M6, and tinkering with retro computers. I live in the East Village with my two cats, Steve and Buscemi.
