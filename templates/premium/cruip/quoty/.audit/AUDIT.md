# Cruip Quoty audit

Reference wrapper: <https://cruip.com/demos/quoty/>

Direct content source: <https://preview.cruip.com/quoty/>

The source and clone both contain home, details, contact, and payment routes. No page was missing or extra. The complete quote breakdown, six cost rows, five terms, details article and figure, contact form, card form, and PayPal panel were checked.

One repair iteration refreshed the complete current HTML, compiled styles, Alpine behavior, images, favicons, and locally vendored fonts. The accordion, payment method, theme persistence, focus styling, validation, hover states, and responsive layouts were replayed.

Final full-page captures had exact dimensions and SSIM from 0.998241 to 0.999143. Twenty-four responsive comparisons covered 390, 768, and 1280 pixel widths in both themes. Every pair had identical dimensions, median SSIM was 0.998003, and minimum SSIM was 0.995277. Five deterministic interaction states ranged from 0.998498 to 1.0.

All four routes converged after one repair iteration with no residual page, section, breakpoint, interaction, or theme gap.
