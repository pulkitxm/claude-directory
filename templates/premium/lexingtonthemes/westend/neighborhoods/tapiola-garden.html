Tapiola Garden

Finland's iconic garden city

Tapiola is a world-famous example of 1950s garden city planning, where architecture and nature exist in perfect harmony. This neighborhood in Espoo features modernist architecture surrounded by forests, with excellent schools, cultural facilities, and direct metro access to Helsinki.
