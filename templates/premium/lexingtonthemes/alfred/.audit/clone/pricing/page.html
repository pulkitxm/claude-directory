Frequently asked questions

Can I try Alfred before buying? +

Yes! Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You can explore all features and decide which plan suits your team best.

What happens when my trial ends? +

When your trial ends, you'll be prompted to choose a plan. Your data is always safe and you can export it at any time.

Can I change my plan later? +

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Is there a discount for annual billing? +

Yes! Annual billing saves you 20% compared to monthly. Contact us to switch to annual billing.
