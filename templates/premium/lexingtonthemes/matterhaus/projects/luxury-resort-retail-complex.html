Project Overview

The Luxury Resort & Retail Complex creates a seamless blend of world-class hospitality and premium retail experiences in a stunning beachfront setting. This 220,000 square foot development combines luxury accommodations, fine dining, and upscale shopping in a design that celebrates the natural beauty of Paradise Bay while providing exceptional guest experiences.

Design Philosophy

Our design approach harmonized luxury hospitality with environmental sensitivity, creating spaces that offer both indulgence and sustainability. The complex incorporates biophilic design principles, local materials, and passive cooling strategies to create a resort that respects its natural surroundings while providing unparalleled comfort.

Key Features

Luxury Hospitality

  • 150 ocean-view guest rooms and suites
  • Spa and wellness center with traditional and modern treatments
  • Multiple dining venues featuring local and international cuisine
  • Infinity pool and beachfront recreation areas

Premium Retail Experience

  • Curated collection of luxury boutiques and galleries
  • Open-air marketplace with local artisans
  • Fine dining establishments and specialty food venues
  • Entertainment and cultural performance spaces

Environmental Integration

  • Living roof systems with native vegetation
  • Natural ventilation and daylighting strategies
  • Water conservation systems and sustainable landscaping
  • Energy-efficient building systems with renewable integration

Technical Specifications

  • Total Area: 220,000 sq ft
  • Rooms: 150 guest rooms and suites
  • Retail Space: 80,000 sq ft of commercial space
  • Parking: Underground with valet services
  • Construction: Reinforced concrete with sustainable materials
  • Completion: Q3 2024

Impact & Recognition

This resort complex has become a destination landmark, attracting visitors from around the world while contributing to local economic development. The project has received international recognition for its innovative approach to luxury hospitality and sustainable tourism, setting new standards for beachfront development.
