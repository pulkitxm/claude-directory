## MOTION / ANIMATION / INTERACTION SPEC

- **SCROLL REVEAL**: ELEMENTS WITH A `.reveal` CLASS START AT `opacity:0; translateY(30px)` AND ANIMATE TO VISIBLE VIA AN INTERSECTIONOBSERVER (THRESHOLD ~0.1), WITH STAGGERED `transition-delay` STEPS INSIDE THE HERO. EASING `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`, ~0.8S.
- **HERO IMAGE SLIDER**: PURE CROSS-FADE (OPACITY) BETWEEN 3 ABSOLUTELY-STACKED SLIDES ON A 5S INTERVAL.
- **LIFESTYLE SLIDER**: 3 SLIDES CROSS-FADE WITH A SMALL HORIZONTAL TRANSLATE EVERY ~6S.
- **TESTIMONIAL TRACK**: TRANSFORM-TRANSLATEX A FLEX TRACK ONE CARD AT A TIME ON A 5S INTERVAL, LOOPING.
- **COUNTDOWN**: A REAL `setInterval` TICKING HH:MM:SS DOWN FROM A FIXED OFFSET.
- **FAQ ACCORDION**: CLICKING A QUESTION CLOSES ALL OTHERS AND TOGGLES MAX-HEIGHT/OPACITY ON ITS CONTENT; CHEVRON ROTATES.
- **HOVER MICRO-INTERACTIONS**: CATEGORY/PRODUCT/BLOG IMAGE ZOOM, NAV CTA COLOR INVERT, PRODUCT TITLE → ORANGE, PHILOSOPHY ICON FLIP + CARD LIFT, ARROW LINKS NUDGE.
- **TOAST**: CLICKING A PRODUCT/OFFER CARD POPS A SMALL BOTTOM-RIGHT INK TOAST ("ADDED TO COLLECTION ✓") THAT SLIDES IN AND AUTO-DISMISSES.
- RESPECT `prefers-reduced-motion`: DISABLE NON-ESSENTIAL TRANSITIONS/AUTOPLAY WHEN SET.
